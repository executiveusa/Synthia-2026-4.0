# Direct outreach pipeline

Main channel for high-value work (W2 landing pages, W3 scroll-world sites, W6 retainers).
The opener is a real finding about the prospect's own site, produced by a short W1 audit.

## Pipeline

1. **Niche.** One niche per campaign, written in `NICHE.md`: who they are, where they
   are, what their site must make them money from. *Not chosen yet — owner decision.*
2. **List.** Businesses in the niche with a live site and a findable contact.
   Record source and date for each. Public business contacts only.
3. **Short audit.** Run W1 `01_inspect` and `02_audit` in prospect mode. Keep only the
   single most costly P0 or P1 finding with its screenshot.
4. **Draft.** A short message: the finding, what it costs them, the fix, one question.
   Run The Extractor on the draft.
5. **Owner sends.** An agent drafts; a human approves and sends each message.
   Cold email must follow the law where the recipient is (e.g. CAN-SPAM in the US:
   real sender identity, a physical address, a working opt-out).
6. **Follow up.** Scheduled, limited, stopped on any opt-out.
7. **Log.** Every send, reply and result in `pricing-tests.md` and `LOG.md`.

Never message the same prospect from two campaigns. Never invent familiarity.
