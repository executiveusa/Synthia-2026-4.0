# Gig draft — W1 Website Trust & Slop Audit

**Title:** I will audit your website and show you what stops visitors from contacting you

**Description:**

You send a link. I check the live site on a phone and a desktop and send back a
report with three things:

1. What a first-time visitor understands in five seconds, and what they miss.
2. Every problem that blocks your main action (booking, buying, calling, applying),
   with a screenshot of each one.
3. The fixes in order, starting with the ones that cost you customers.

Each finding comes with evidence: a screenshot, the exact words on the page, or a
measurement. No generic checklists.

**What I need from you:** your site link and the one thing you want visitors to do.

**Packages:** price, scope and delivery time to be set from `sales/pricing-tests.md`.

**Gallery:** before/after case studies from W1 `08_learn`, with client permission. None yet.

**FAQ**
- *Do you change my site?* No. This is an audit. If you want the fixes made, I quote them separately.
- *Do you need my login?* No. I review what your visitors see.
