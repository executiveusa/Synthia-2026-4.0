# Gig draft — W7 Social Commercial (up to 30 s)

**Title:** I will make a 15 or 30 second social ad for your product, ready for Reels, TikTok and Shorts

**Description:**

You get one short ad built around one message and one action, sized for every
platform you run on.

What you choose:
- **Creator style:** a presenter shows your product, unboxes it, tries it on or walks through it.
- **Product style:** your product is the star, with a voiceover and motion graphics.
- **Software style:** your real screens animated with your fonts and colors.

How it works: we agree the message → you get a script and three directions → you pick
one and approve the budget → draft → your changes → final files in 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9 as ordered.

Every spoken word is captioned. Nothing important sits under the platform buttons.

**What I need from you:** product link or photos, logo, brand colors, the claims you can back up,
and the one thing viewers should do.

**Honest rules:** presenters may be AI-generated. They show your product; they never
pretend to be customers or give reviews. Claims only if you can prove them.

**Packages:** length, formats, revisions, variants and price to be set from `sales/pricing-tests.md`.
