# 00_lock — lock the ad before anything is made

One job: agree the one message, the one action, the limits and the budget ceiling.

## Inputs
- Client order
- `skills/grill-me/SKILL.md`
- `_factory/canon/SPINE.md` §00_lock
- `_factory/video/COMMERCIAL_DOCTRINE.md` (hard limits, truth rules)

Do NOT load: later stages; site workflows.

## Process
1. Run grill-me. Spine lock fields plus: length (6, 15 or 30 s; never over 30), every aspect ratio ordered, platforms, paid or organic, objective (sales, leads, traffic, awareness), the offer, the one action, sound or silent, voiceover language(s), budget ceiling in Higgsfield credits and dollars.
2. Talent: generated creator, client's own people (written consent required), or no people.
3. AI disclosure plan: which parts will be AI-generated and how the client will label them.
4. Write the one message in one sentence and the action in five words or fewer.

## Outputs
- LOCK.md → output/

## Human check
Read the message aloud. If it needs a second sentence, it is not locked. Confirm the budget ceiling in writing.
