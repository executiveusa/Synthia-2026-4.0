# W7 — Social Commercial (up to 30 s)

**Sells as:** a finished 6, 15 or 30 s commercial for social, in every ordered aspect ratio,
optionally with variants (markets, languages) and a static ad set.
**Lanes:** code-rendered, UGC creator, cinematic product, static ads, assembly, variants.
See `_factory/video/COMMERCIAL_DOCTRINE.md`.
**Rubric profile:** `commercial`.

Stages: `00_lock → 01_inspect → 02_research → 03_direction → 04_build → 05_gauntlet → 06_approval → 07_deliver → 08_learn`

Two money gates: no paid generation before the budget is approved in `03_direction`,
no finals or variants before `06_approval`.

Upwork milestones: (1) lock + script + treatments + budget at 03, (2) approved draft at 06, (3) finals at 07.
