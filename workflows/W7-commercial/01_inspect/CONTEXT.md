# 01_inspect — collect what is real and provable

One job: gather the real product, brand and the claims the client can prove.

## Inputs
- ../00_lock/output/LOCK.md
- `_factory/video/COMMERCIAL_DOCTRINE.md` (truth rules)

Do NOT load: reference ads (that is 02).

## Process
1. Collect product photos or the product page, logo, fonts, colors, existing footage, approved copy.
2. Claims allowlist: every claim the client wants, marked PROVEN (with the evidence) or REMOVE.
3. Licenses: fonts, music, footage, talent releases. Record each.
4. Optional, if the client has a website: Higgsfield `ads_studio_create_brand` researches brand kit and products. Check whether it spends credits before calling; if it does, it needs owner approval like any paid call.

## Outputs
- ASSETS.md → output/
- CLAIMS.md (allowlist) → output/
- LICENSES.md → output/

## Human check
Every line in CLAIMS.md marked PROVEN has evidence attached. Delete the rest.
