## Conflict resolution
Preserve task requirements and source truth before style preferences. Stop Slop's blanket deletion of adverbs, passive voice, and dashes is a useful aggressive editing signal, not permission to erase meaning or the author's voice. Humanizer's voice and fact-preservation rules win when a pattern is intentional. Ponytail applies to code, not to flattening prose. The owner-approved design system wins over generic redesign instructions. The source's own required legal, accessibility, security, and approval gates remain intact.
