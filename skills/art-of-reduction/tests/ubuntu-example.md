# Ubuntu reduction test

## Input and provenance
Human-authored source: the owner's saved personal-note draft, signed Jeremy Bowers, Co-Creator — MACS Digital Media. The source identifies the meeting at the ASC3ND barbecue and describes the research performed.

Original excerpt:
> Over the last week, I took some time to really look through Ubuntu — the website, menu, reviews, ordering experience, catering, social media and search presence. I studied the competition around you and the systems I can publicly see already connected to your business.

AI-authored comparison from an earlier assistant:
> This brief is not about telling you that something is broken. It is simply a few things we noticed, what already appears to be working, and where we believe there may be room to test something useful.

## Audit
Object: personal owner letter, not an SEO report.
Audience: Ubuntu's owner.
Outcome: establish that Jeremy did the homework and invite her perspective.
Protected: named meeting, scope of research, accurate uncertainty, authorship, actual signoff.
Flags: “not about ... broken” is a defensive contrast; “a few things we noticed” is vague; “room to test something useful” is an abstract ending. In the human paragraph, “I took some time to really” is optional but not a sign of AI authorship.
Decision: retain the human observation; remove the AI framing. Move detailed platform evidence to the report instead of placing it all in the letter.

## Proposed reduction (R2)
> Over the last week, I looked through Ubuntu's website, menu, reviews, ordering and catering, along with its social presence and the competition around you. I also looked at the systems I could see publicly.

This is a proposed edit, not an approved replacement. The original remains available.
